> Most people learn market making by reading Avellaneda-Stoikov (2008) or looking at static charts. I wanted an interactive, tactile arena where you actually feel the pain of adverse selection and toxic order flow in real-time.

> • Live inventory risk-shading sliders (tweak gamma and spread to protect against informed sweeps)
